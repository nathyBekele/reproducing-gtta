SA Inspector live

Syntactic Alignment (SA) adds a small learned vector δ to a model's last layer, trained for a step or two on the prompt, before the model answers. This page runs it live on a GPU: pick a task or paste your own text, press Run SA, and see two things for yourself: with the released code, SA's step gets smaller the longer the prompt is, and the words it boosts are mostly the prompt's most common ones, like punctuation and repeated phrases.

How SA is computed here: as in the GTTA code (r2llab/GTTA, commit 24ba304). δ is added after the model's final layer norm and trained with Adam (β 0.9 / 0.95, ε 10⁻⁶, weight decay 10⁻⁵) on the next-token loss of the prompt. Runs are rate-limited.
